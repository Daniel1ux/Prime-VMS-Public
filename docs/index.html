The essentials of a VMS

Live view, recording, playback and export are included in every edition. The same software scales from a single computer to several servers.

Works with your cameras

Finds ONVIF cameras on the network and detects the RTSP stream path. You only enter the camera user name and password.

Continuous recording

Records to disk in segmented MP4 without re-encoding, with a recording catalog and retention by days.

Live view

Layouts up to 4×4, full screen, multiple monitors and video wall, with hardware-accelerated decoding.

Playback

Up to 16 cameras on one clock, reverse playback, motion search and signed export.

Export

Export images and video with a watermark that includes your logo, date and time.

Users and roles

Administrator, supervisor and operator roles, enforced by the server.

Distributed deployment

Run everything on one PC, or use a central server with operator stations. Multiple recording and AI servers are part of Professional.

Updates from the app

Only changed files are downloaded, usually about 1 MB. Releases are signed, and a failed update rolls back automatically.

How it works

One installer with three components. Install them on one PC or across your network.

Storage

The recording server. Pulls the RTSP stream from each camera, records to disk, applies retention and serves live and recorded video.

System Manager

The central server. Holds cameras, users and permissions, and assigns each camera to a recording server.

Workstation

The operator application. Live layouts, playback timeline, multiple monitors and export.

Frequently asked questions

Is Nexa VMS free?

The Free edition costs nothing and has no time limit. It covers up to 16 cameras on one Windows site, with up to 3 days of recording and an AI hours quota. Basic and Professional are licensed per camera, either as a perpetual license with 1 year of maintenance or as a subscription. Prices in US dollars are at nexavms.io/precos.

Do I need to activate the installation?

Yes. You activate the installation from the System Manager Console with a Nexa VMS account created at nexavms.io. Until it is activated, the system does not record or show video. The first activation includes a 30-day Professional trial. After that, the installation returns to Free unless you buy a license.

Which cameras are supported?

Any IP camera with ONVIF or RTSP. Nexa VMS finds ONVIF cameras on the network and detects the stream path.

Can it replace an NVR?

Nexa VMS records IP cameras directly on a Windows PC or server, with retention by days. It can also record from existing recorders that provide RTSP streams.

How many cameras can I add?

Up to 16 on Free and up to 32 on Basic. Professional is licensed per camera, up to 500 per server, with multiple Storage and AI Servers and a standby server (N+1). The Enterprise edition, with high availability, is coming soon.

How is AI licensed?

Video analytics (people counting, face recognition, license plates, smart search and perimeter protection) are licensed per channel, meaning per camera and module, or as site-wide AI. Free includes an AI hours quota. On Free, face recognition is available only in demo mode and during the trial.

Which languages are available?

English, Portuguese (Brazil) and Spanish, in the apps, the installer and the release notes.

How do I open a protected export (.pvx)?

Protected exports are encrypted with a password and only open in Nexa Player, free, for Windows. Download Nexa Player, extract the zip, open PrimePlayer.exe, choose the .pvx file and enter the password. Nexa Player shows whether each video is intact and signed.
